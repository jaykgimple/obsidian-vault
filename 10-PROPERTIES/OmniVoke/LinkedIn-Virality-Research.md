# What makes a LinkedIn short go viral

5-agent deep research (deepseek-v4-pro), 5 angles, 69 sources. Compiled 2026-11-23.

## The one thing to know first

Video is the WEAKEST organic format on LinkedIn. Carousels/documents win reach (1.39x) and engagement (21.77% median vs 7.35% video, Buffer 45M posts). Video reach fell ~36% YoY. So a Short's job is personality + trust + hook-driven attention, not raw reach. Optimize the hook, keep it under 30s, caption everything.

## 1. Hooks (first 1.3 seconds)

- Scroll decision in 1.3s, not 3s (Meta 2025 eye-tracking). 68% gone by 3s, 87% by 8s, 95% by 15s.
- Winning openers: imperative ("Stop doing X"), how-to ("How to X in Y"), specific-number ("$1,300", "147 rejections").
- Losing openers: questions (0.93x), generic greetings ("Hey everyone" = 23% lower retention), vague clickbait.
- Sound-off feed: hook must work as on-screen text + first frame, not audio. Visual/motion + bold text overlay = the pattern interrupt.

## 2. Format & length

- <30s for awareness; <30s = 200% higher completion. Median watch time 5.86s (Vidpros).
- Captions mandatory (67% watch muted).
- Front-load the point in first 10s.
- Talking-head = ~half the reach of a static image. Video for personality/demo; carousels for frameworks.
- Hook <40 chars, <10 words. Carousels only pay off past ~10 pages.

## 3. Psychology

- High-arousal emotions (anger, awe, humor) out-share low-arousal (sadness, contentment), regardless of sign.
- Self-relevance + identity-signaling drive shares (people share what reflects who they are).
- Vulnerability framed as "triumph over tragedy" = 4.2x over polished success.
- Subjectivity (31.7% of virality) matters far more than sentiment polarity (4.6%).

## 4. Algorithm

- Two-stage: LLM retrieval + transformer (Generative Recommender) ranking. Scores dwell + click/viral probability.
- Levers: dwell time, comment depth (replies), saves (~5x a like). Likes near-irrelevant.
- Early engagement is real (staged distribution), but "90 minutes" is folklore, not official.
- Hashtags de-emphasized (following removed 2024). Creator Mode retired; Follow-first + Top Voices are the levers.

## 5. Data & case studies

- Top topics: meta-content (LinkedIn about LinkedIn) 1.61-1.72x, AI 1.72x, HR/job-search 1.54x.
- Small accounts (<1K) have the highest viral multiple (82x).
- Named wins: Dapper "Wolf of Wall Street but B2B" (5M impressions), Julia McCoy 4s facepalm (77K views), Lanny Heiz $330K-spent hook (313K impressions, $100K revenue).

## OmniVoke playbook

1. Hook-first: specific-number or imperative hook in the first frame as text (sound-off).
2. <30s, captions on, point in first 10s.
3. High-arousal + self-relevant + specific. Avoid questions + generic greetings.
4. Topic targeting: AI, career/HR, meta-content overperform.
5. Position Shorts for personality/trust + hook-driven attention; pair with a carousel for reach.
