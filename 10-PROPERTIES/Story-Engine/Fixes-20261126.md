# Story Engine Fixes — 2026-11-26 (run completion: "The Bloodline Heist" / series 7)

Series 7 ("The Bloodline Heist", book id 60 in Supabase) completed and pushed. 13/13 chapters, 52 segments, avg quality 9.1. Verified in BOTH SQLite and Supabase (13 chapters, 13 with content, 13 completed).

## Root causes fixed (two compounding engine bugs, both surgical)

### 1. Judge token cap (`agents/fact_checker.py`)
- `judge_issue()` called the continuity judge with `max_tokens=400`. Arbitrating 35 facts needs more; the judge's JSON truncated (`finish_reason=length`), `json.loads` failed, returned `error=True` → full segment retry. Fired every 30-60s.
- Fix: `400 → 2048` (the trigger for the endless "fact-checker judge unverifiable → retry" noise).

### 2. Cumulative error abort (`pipeline/engine.py`)
- `run_all` hard-aborted on `len(errors) >= 20`, counting every transient "judge unverifiable → retry" *cumulatively* across the whole multi-hour run. 20 scattered recoverable glitches = false full-book abort.
- Fix: abort on 20 **consecutive** errors (reset on any success), not 20 cumulative. Only a genuinely-stuck segment trips it now.

## Self-healing behavior confirmed (worked as designed)
- A segment that survives revision with a real LLM-confirmed contradiction is marked status `error` ("marked for review") and NOT shipped. `_reconcile_cursor` treats `error` as complete to avoid infinite retry, but `_qc_scan_and_fix` (run at `run_all` startup) force-regenerates `error` segments with their stored issues.
- ch9seg3 was flagged one run, fixed the next. ch8seg3 (novelist hallucinated Beth into "Flamingo Casino, room 1412" Vegas during a Flagstaff-library chapter; only belongs to ch5-7) was flagged, then regenerated clean on the following restart.
