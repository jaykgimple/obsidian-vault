# Agentic Revision: How Autonomous Systems Improve Their Own Output Without Human Intervention

> Part of → [[10-PROPERTIES/OctoGentic/Blog-Index|Blog Index]] ← [[10-PROPERTIES/OctoGentic/Overview|OctoGentic]]

## Thesis

Revision is the capability that separates systems that ship adequate first drafts from systems that ship good output. It is a structured loop (generate, evaluate, gap analyze, surgically revise, re-evaluate) that runs without human intervention and is governed by explicit stopping conditions.

## Key Takeaways

- **T-RV1: Gap analysis must be specific to be actionable.** "This output is bad" produces blind rerolls. Specific gap analysis produces targeted revision.
- **T-RV2: Regenerate only the failing parts.** Wholesale revision wastes budget and breaks what already works. Surgical revision guided by gap analysis is how production systems improve without regressing.
- **T-RV3: Evaluate progressively across all dimensions after every revision.** A change that improves one dimension may degrade another. Score everything every time.
- **T-RV4: Set explicit stopping conditions with multiple exit paths.** Quality threshold and ship. Diminishing returns and ship with a flag. Budget exhausted and escalate.
- **T-RV5: Revision is the quality multiplier.** The first draft establishes the floor. Revision raises the ceiling. Each revision teaches the system about the gap between its first attempt and the standard.
