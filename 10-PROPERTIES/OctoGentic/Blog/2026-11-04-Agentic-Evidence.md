# Agentic Evidence: How Autonomous Systems Keep Claims Proportional to Proof

> Part of → [[10-PROPERTIES/OctoGentic/Blog-Index|Blog Index]] ← [[10-PROPERTIES/OctoGentic/Overview|OctoGentic]]
> Published: 2026-11-04

## Thesis

A system can be grounded, calibrated, and verified, and still overclaim. Evidence proportionality binds every claim to the specific evidence that supports it, so a signal may move only the dimensions it actually touches.

## Key Takeaways

- **T-EP1: Bind every claim to its evidence.** A claim is a function of specific inputs, not of general confidence. Record which evidence sources may move each output field.
- **T-EP2: Update only the dimensions a signal supports.** Correlation is not causation, and convenience is not justification. Scoped updates prevent a strong answer from inflating scores it has nothing to do with.
- **T-EP3: Surface negative evidence.** Absence of proof is data. Flag what the source does not support instead of smoothing it into a vague strength.
- **T-EP4: Audit proportionality after generation.** A separate pass checks that no claim exceeds its reference. Unreferenced claims are downgraded, qualified, or removed.
- **T-EP5: Treat coherence as an output quality, not a source of truth.** A plausible story is not evidence. When the source is silent, the system says so.

## Properties Referenced

- [[10-PROPERTIES/RoleFresh|RoleFresh]]: Intake-grounded details raise only the quantified impact dimension, with no bullet-strength or ATS pollution, no fabricated delta, and no mutation of the raw resume text. The parser preserves evidence instead of interpreting it. Red flags explain why a reviewer would pass and are injected into every rewrite prompt as elimination targets.
- [[10-PROPERTIES/OmniVoke|OmniVoke]]: One input becomes platform-native content across every connected channel. The source is the evidence boundary, so reach scales without claim strength scaling.
