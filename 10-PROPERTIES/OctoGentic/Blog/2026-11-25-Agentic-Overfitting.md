# Agentic Overfitting: Why Tailoring Too Hard to a Target Makes a System Less Fit, Not More

> Part of → [[10-PROPERTIES/OctoGentic/Blog-Index|Blog Index]] ← [[10-PROPERTIES/OctoGentic/Overview|OctoGentic]]
> Published: 2026-11-25

## Thesis

Overfitting, borrowed from machine learning, is the divergence between the metric a system is told to optimize and the outcome it is meant to produce. In any tailoring system (a resume matched to a posting, a pitch tuned to a buyer) the measured proxy can climb while the real goal falls away. The overfit resume mirrors the job description so perfectly that it wins the machine and loses the human: perfect keyword overlap is table stakes, not a signal, and the human reads a resume written from the posting rather than from a career. The fix is fidelity as a hard constraint, not a soft preference. Ground tailoring in the source of truth (the master resume), make "do not invent employers or metrics" inviolable, and treat the target score as a floor to clear rather than a target to maximize. Optimization happens inside a boundary that preserves the truth.

## Key Takeaways

- **T-OF1: Overfitting is metric-goal divergence.** When the number a system is told to raise separates from the outcome it is meant to produce, raising the number erodes the outcome. The score climbs while the purpose falls away.
- **T-OF2: A proxy pushed past its use hollows out the real thing.** The overfit resume maximizes keyword overlap by stripping the true details that made the candidate distinct. It disappears to the machine and reads as hollow to the human.
- **T-OF3: Fidelity is a hard constraint, not a soft preference.** Ground tailoring in the source of truth and make "do not invent employers or metrics" inviolable. Optimization happens inside a boundary that preserves truth.
- **T-OF4: A target score is a floor to clear, not a target to maximize.** Clear the ATS match, then remain exactly as true as the source. A resume that clears the floor and stays faithful is fit; one that maxes the score by leaving the source behind is overfit.

## Properties Referenced

- [[10-PROPERTIES/RoleFresh|RoleFresh]]: The AI job search platform tailors resumes to job descriptions. Its rewriting agent is grounded in the candidate's master resume (truth grounding: never invent employers or metrics), so tailoring operates inside a fidelity boundary. The ATS match score is a floor to clear rather than a target to chase. The failure mode the constraint prevents is the overfit resume that mirrors the posting's keywords so perfectly it reads as hollow to the human reviewer and disappears into the noise of a hundred equally keyword-stuffed applications.
