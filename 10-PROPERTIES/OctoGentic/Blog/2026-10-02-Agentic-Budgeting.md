## Thesis

Every autonomous system faces finite resources and infinite demands. Budgeting is the architecture that decides who gets what, when to stop, and how to recover when the allocation was wrong. Three mechanisms: budget definition, real-time tracking, and adaptive reallocation.

## Key Takeaways

- **T-B1**: Define budgets before action begins. Every agent needs a clearly defined, durably stored budget with maximum consumption, time window, and priority level.
- **T-B2**: Track spend in real time. Budget tracking must be accurate, low-overhead, and visible at every level.
- **T-B3**: Reallocate based on priority and return. Budgets flow to the highest-priority, highest-return tasks, and away from overspenders.
- **T-B4**: Build recovery mechanisms for overspend. Throttling, termination, and budget extension must be in place before they are needed.
- **T-B5**: Make budget tradeoffs visible to users. A simplified result delivered within budget is better than a timeout error, but only if the user understands what was cut and why.
