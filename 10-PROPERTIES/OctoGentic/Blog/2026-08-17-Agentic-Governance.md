## Summary

Governance isn't a constraint on agentic power — it's the architecture that makes sustained autonomy possible. Three pillars: Decision Rights Architecture, Audit Trail Integrity, Alignment Verification.

## Key Takeaways

- **T-GV1: Architect Decision Rights Explicitly** — Classify every decision type by reversibility, impact, and stakeholder sensitivity. Define explicit governance requirements for each.
- **T-GV2: Build Tamper-Evident Audit Trails** — Append-only, cryptographically verifiable audit storage. Every decision must be reconstructable.
- **T-GV3: Verify Alignment Continuously** — Static alignment at launch is insufficient. Monitor behavior against intent continuously.
- **T-GV4: Deploy Dedicated Governance Agents** — Separate concern requiring dedicated agents. The immune system of the portfolio.
- **T-GV5: Close the Governance Compounding Loop** — Every governance finding feeds back into agent behavior. Governance that doesn't compound decays.

## Topics Covered

- The governance gap (operational effectiveness vs decision legitimacy)
- Decision rights architecture (reversibility, impact, sensitivity)
- Audit trail integrity (completeness, tamper-evidence, queryability)
- Alignment verification (static, dynamic, adversarial)
- The governance compounding loop
