# Agentic Framing: How Autonomous Systems Decide What to Emphasize and What to Leave Out

> Part of → [[10-PROPERTIES/OctoGentic/Blog-Index|Blog Index]] ← [[10-PROPERTIES/OctoGentic/Overview|OctoGentic]]
> Published: 2026-10-27

## Thesis

Framing is the capability that lets autonomous systems adapt the presentation of the same underlying reality to serve a specific context without changing the facts. It is not personalization. Personalization adapts the message to the user. Framing adapts the presentation to the context.

## Key Takeaways

- **T-FR1: Salience is a ranking decision.** Every output must decide what leads, what details, and what omits. Flat presentation (equal weight to all facts) is the failure mode of systems without framing judgment.
- **T-FR2: Proportional emphasis preserves truth.** Good framing emphasizes what matters most without distorting what matters least. Over-framing (burying, rewriting) becomes fabrication.
- **T-FR3: Negative framing constraints prevent distortion.** Explicit rules (never omit material context, never rewrite facts) bound the framing engine so it cannot optimize for engagement at the expense of accuracy.
- **T-FR4: Framing audit validates the output.** Compare the framed presentation against the source reality. If a reader who sees only the framed output would make a materially different decision than one who saw the full context, the framing failed.

## Properties Referenced

- [[10-PROPERTIES/RoleFresh|RoleFresh]]: Resume and profile framing. Every role gets emphasis proportional to relevance, not recency. Negative constraints prevent burying gaps or inflating titles.
