## Thesis

Autonomous systems constantly make commitments: to users, to other agents, to external services. A contract is the architecture that turns those promises into verifiable obligations. Three mechanisms: explicit commitment encoding, fulfillment verification, and breach handling.

## Key Takeaways

- **T-C1**: Encode commitments explicitly. Every commitment needs an identifier, a success condition, a deadline, and a consequence.
- **T-C2**: Verify fulfillment by checking external state, not just internal action logs.
- **T-C3**: Define breach consequences that are proportional and visible.
- **T-C4**: Track contract fulfillment over time as a compounding reliability signal.
- **T-C5**: Make contracts visible to users so trust is built on transparency.
